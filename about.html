WHO WE ARE

A team passionate about workplace safety

InnovateX is a software company established by six students with a shared interest in improving workplace safety and wellness. We develop interactive digital training solutions that help organisations move beyond passive training methods and deliver learning experiences employees actually engage with.

Our focus is on creating realistic, scenario-based training that helps employees recognise hazards, make safe decisions, and retain what they learn.
